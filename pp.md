# Privacy Policy for prediction-league

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Overview
- prediction-league (“the App”) operates entirely within the Reddit platform. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your data responsibly.

2. Information Collected
- The App processes only data necessary for its core functionality, including:

    - Reddit User ID and Username: Used to associate predictions, leaderboards, win streaks, and inventory.
    - In-App Activity: Prediction choices, points, leaderboards, and streak statistics.
    - Purchase Data: Transaction confirmations provided by Reddit’s Payments API to fulfill digital items.

3. Data Storage & Security
- All app data is stored using Reddit’s native Devvit infrastructure. We do not operate external data storage systems for the App.

4. Contact
- For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via Reddit message or through our official support site (if available).
