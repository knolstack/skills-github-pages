Privacy Policy for LG Smart Remote: webOS TV
Effective Date: October 13, 2026

At LG Smart Remote: webOS TV ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information when you use our app to control your LG webOS Smart TV.

1. Information Collection and Local Network Usage
No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, names, email addresses, or user profiles.

Local Network Communication: The app communicates locally over your private Wi-Fi network to connect directly to your LG Smart TV. It does not route your remote commands or TV data through any external cloud servers.

Local Device Storage: The app saves your previously connected TV’s IP address and local pairing keys (Client Keys) strictly on your device using Android’s secure storage systems (SharedPreferences and hardware-backed KeyStore) so you do not have to re-pair every time you open the app. This data never leaves your device.

2. Permissions Required
To function correctly, the app requests minimal, local-only permissions:

Wi-Fi / Network State Access: Used exclusively to discover and connect to your LG Smart TV on your local Wi-Fi subnet.

Internet Access: Used solely to establish secure local WebSockets (wss://) with your TV’s IP address.

3. Third-Party Services
Our app does not integrate any third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data-collection tools. All financial transactions (if applicable) are handled securely and directly by Google Play Billing, subject to Google’s privacy policies.

4. Children’s Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since the app is a local utility tool, it contains no age-restricted content.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
